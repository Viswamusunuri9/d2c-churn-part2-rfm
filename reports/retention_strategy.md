# Retention Strategy Report

## D2C Customer Churn Intelligence Project
### Part 2 - RFM Segmentation & Retention Strategy

---

# Executive Summary

A customer segmentation framework was developed using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) analysis. Customers were grouped into six actionable segments based on purchasing behavior and customer value.

The objective of the segmentation exercise is to enable targeted retention actions rather than applying uniform campaigns across the entire customer base.

The analysis identified 2,400 customers distributed across six segments with varying retention priorities and expected business value.

# Segment Profiles

## 1. Champions

### Characteristics

- Lowest recency levels
- Highest purchase frequency
- Highest spending behavior
- Strongest customer engagement

### Business Value

Champions represent the company's most valuable customer group and contribute disproportionately to revenue generation.

### Recommended Actions

- VIP loyalty rewards
- Early access to new products
- Exclusive member-only offers
- Referral and advocacy programs

### Success Metric

- Retention Rate
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
- Referral Participation

---

## 2. Loyal Customers

### Characteristics

- Consistent purchasing behavior
- Strong engagement patterns
- Moderate-to-high spending

### Business Value

These customers form the core of the customer base and represent stable recurring revenue.

### Recommended Actions

- Loyalty point acceleration
- Personalized product recommendations
- Subscription or replenishment programs
- Cross-sell and upsell campaigns

### Success Metric

- Repeat Purchase Rate
- Average Order Value
- Loyalty Program Participation

---

## 3. Potential Loyalists

### Characteristics

- Very recent purchases
- Limited purchase history
- Moderate spending behavior

### Business Value

These customers represent future growth opportunities and can be converted into loyal customers with relatively low intervention costs.

### Recommended Actions

- Welcome journeys
- Product education campaigns
- Personalized recommendations
- Second-purchase incentives

### Success Metric

- Second Purchase Conversion Rate
- Purchase Frequency Growth
- Segment Migration Rate

---

## 4. At Risk

### Characteristics

- High historical value
- Previously active customers
- Significant increase in recency
- Declining engagement

### Business Value

This segment represents the highest retention opportunity because these customers previously generated meaningful revenue.

### Recommended Actions

- Win-back campaigns
- Personalized retention offers
- Re-engagement emails
- Customer success outreach

### Success Metric

- Reactivation Rate
- Revenue Recovered
- Churn Reduction

### Priority Level

**Highest Priority**

---

## 5. Needs Attention

### Characteristics

- Moderate engagement
- Moderate value
- Signs of declining activity

### Business Value

These customers may transition into either loyal or at-risk groups depending on future engagement.

### Recommended Actions

- Engagement reminders
- Product discovery campaigns
- Behavioral nudges

### Success Metric

- Engagement Rate
- Segment Migration

---

## 6. Hibernating

### Characteristics

- Very high recency
- Very low frequency
- Lowest spending levels

### Business Value

These customers currently provide limited business value and may not justify expensive retention efforts.

### Recommended Actions

- Low-cost email campaigns
- Automated reactivation journeys
- Seasonal promotions

### Success Metric

- Reactivation Rate
- Campaign ROI

---

# Retention Priority Framework

Given limited retention resources, customer segments should be prioritized according to expected business impact and recovery potential.

| Priority | Segment | Rationale |
|----------|----------|----------|
| 1 | At Risk | High value with strong recovery potential |
| 2 | Potential Loyalists | High future growth potential |
| 3 | Loyal Customers | Prevent migration into At Risk |
| 4 | Champions | Maintain loyalty and advocacy |
| 5 | Needs Attention | Monitor engagement trends |
| 6 | Hibernating | Low-cost reactivation only |

---

# Budget-Constrained Retention Strategy

Assuming limited retention resources:

### Tier 1 Investment

Focus on:

- At Risk Customers
- Potential Loyalists

Expected Outcome:

- Maximum retention ROI
- Revenue preservation
- Reduced future churn

---

### Tier 2 Investment

Focus on:

- Loyal Customers
- Champions

Expected Outcome:

- Customer lifetime value growth
- Increased advocacy
- Stronger loyalty

---

### Tier 3 Investment

Focus on:

- Needs Attention
- Hibernating

Expected Outcome:

- Opportunistic reactivation
- Incremental engagement improvements

---

# Recommended KPIs

The following KPIs should be monitored after campaign deployment:

| KPI | Objective |
|----------|----------|
| Churn Rate | Measure overall retention improvement |
| Reactivation Rate | Measure win-back campaign effectiveness |
| Repeat Purchase Rate | Measure customer engagement |
| Average Order Value | Measure customer value growth |
| Loyalty Participation Rate | Measure loyalty program effectiveness |
| Segment Migration Rate | Measure movement toward higher-value segments |

---

# Final Recommendation

The analysis indicates that retention efforts should focus primarily on customers who have historically demonstrated value but are exhibiting signs of disengagement.

The At Risk segment represents the most immediate retention opportunity, while Potential Loyalists represent the strongest future growth opportunity. By prioritizing these segments and tailoring interventions according to customer behavior, the company can improve retention efficiency and maximize the return on retention investment.
