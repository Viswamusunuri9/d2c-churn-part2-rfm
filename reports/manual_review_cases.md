# Manual Review Cases

## D2C Customer Churn Intelligence Project
### Part 2 - RFM Segmentation & Retention Strategy

---

# Objective

While RFM segmentation provides an effective framework for customer prioritization, certain customers may require manual review before retention resources are allocated.

This document identifies customer scenarios where automated segment assignment may not fully capture business context.

---

# Why Manual Review Is Necessary

Segmentation models simplify customer behavior into predefined groups. However, some customers exhibit characteristics that may require additional business investigation before intervention decisions are made.

Examples include:

- unusually high-value customers,
- conflicting engagement signals,
- recent retention interventions,
- exceptional purchasing behavior,
- incomplete customer profiles.

---

# Case 1: High-Value At Risk Customers

### Description

Customers classified as **At Risk** who historically generated significant revenue.

### Risk

Losing these customers may result in disproportionate revenue impact.

### Review Recommendation

Before launching automated retention campaigns:

- review purchase history,
- review support interactions,
- review previous retention offers,
- review customer lifetime value.

### Suggested Action

Assign customer success or CRM teams to review these customers individually.

---

# Case 2: Champions Showing Sudden Engagement Decline

### Description

Customers currently classified as Champions but displaying signs of declining web activity or purchasing frequency.

### Risk

These customers may migrate into At Risk segments if engagement continues to decline.

### Review Recommendation

Monitor:

- session activity,
- purchase cadence,
- campaign response behavior.

### Suggested Action

Proactive outreach before disengagement becomes severe.

---

# Case 3: Potential Loyalists With Strong Early Spending

### Description

Customers with limited purchase history but unusually high spending levels.

### Risk

Automated segmentation may underestimate their future value.

### Review Recommendation

Assess:

- category preferences,
- product mix,
- acquisition channel.

### Suggested Action

Provide onboarding and loyalty incentives designed to encourage repeat purchasing.

---

# Case 4: Customers With Repeated Support Interactions

### Description

Customers generating multiple support tickets within a short period.

### Risk

Although support engagement can indicate activity, unresolved issues may eventually contribute to churn.

### Review Recommendation

Review:

- ticket categories,
- resolution times,
- customer satisfaction indicators.

### Suggested Action

Escalate unresolved issues to customer success teams.

---

# Case 5: Recent Retention Campaign Recipients

### Description

Customers who recently received retention interventions.

### Risk

Additional campaigns may create communication fatigue or unnecessary incentive costs.

### Review Recommendation

Evaluate:

- intervention history,
- campaign response rates,
- time since last outreach.

### Suggested Action

Delay further interventions until campaign effectiveness can be measured.

---

# Case 6: Incomplete Customer Profiles

### Description

Customers with missing loyalty information or incomplete profile attributes.

### Risk

Incomplete customer information may reduce segmentation accuracy.

### Review Recommendation

Review:

- loyalty enrollment status,
- customer profile completeness,
- marketing consent status.

### Suggested Action

Encourage profile completion through targeted customer engagement initiatives.

---

# Recommended Review Queue

Given limited analyst capacity, manual review should focus on the following order:

| Priority | Customer Group |
|----------|----------|
| 1 | High-Value At Risk Customers |
| 2 | Champions Showing Declining Activity |
| 3 | Potential Loyalists With High Spend |
| 4 | Customers With Repeated Support Issues |
| 5 | Recent Campaign Recipients |
| 6 | Incomplete Customer Profiles |

---

# Final Recommendation

Automated segmentation should remain the primary decision-making framework. However, selected customer groups should be reviewed manually when the potential business impact justifies additional investigation.

Manual review is particularly important for high-value customers and customers exhibiting conflicting behavioral signals, where the cost of incorrect intervention decisions may be significant.
